# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

This page answers the most common questions about Fortnite Sprite Locker.

---

# 📋 General

## Does Fortnite Sprite Locker require an internet connection?

No. The application works completely offline after downloading.

---

## Do I need to install anything?

No.

Simply open **index.html** in your browser.

---

## Does the application require an account?

No.

There are no accounts, registrations or logins.

---

## Is my collection stored online?

No.

Everything is stored locally in your browser using LocalStorage.

---

# 💾 Saving

## Where is my collection saved?

Inside your browser using LocalStorage.

---

## What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your LocalStorage will be removed.

It is recommended to create regular JSON backups.

---

## Can I move my collection to another computer?

Yes.

Export a JSON backup and import it on the new device.

---

# 📤 Export & Sharing

## Can I export my collection?

Yes.

Supported export formats include:

- PNG
- JSON

---

## Can I share my collection?

Yes.

Use the **Share Collection** feature to generate a shareable URL.

---

## Does sharing upload my data?

No.

Everything is encoded directly into the generated URL.

No external servers are used.

---

# 🛠️ Customization

## Can I add new Sprites?

Yes.

See **CUSTOMIZATION.md** for detailed instructions.

---

## Can I change the interface colors?

Absolutely.

Most colors are controlled through CSS.

---

## Can I replace the images?

Yes.

Replace the corresponding files inside:

```text
assets/sprites/
```

---

# 📱 Compatibility

## Does it work on mobile?

Yes.

The interface is fully responsive.

---

## Which browsers are supported?

- Chrome
- Edge
- Firefox
- Opera
- Safari

---

# 🐞 Troubleshooting

## Images are not loading

Verify that the `assets/` folder has not been moved or renamed.

---

## Export doesn't work

Wait until all Sprite images have finished loading before exporting.

---

## My collection disappeared

Restore your latest JSON backup if available.
